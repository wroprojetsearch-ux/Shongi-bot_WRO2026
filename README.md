ShongiBot - Cultural Companion Robot
Updated Project Proposal
Project Team: Circuit Breakers
Omor - Team Leader | Wadi - Team Member | Mahir - Team Member
1. Project Summary
ShongiBot is a cultural companion robot that engages with humans, answers questions, communicates with voices, and introduces cultural elements of Bangladesh.

The project involves robotics, AI, voice and cultural interaction, and is combined into one fascinating robot.
The robot is built as a standalone autonomous platform, with the possibility of future addition of mobility, including obstacle avoidance.
2. Key Objectives
• Create a cultural companion robot.
• Use AI to answer questions using the robot’s voice.
• Engage with the user in Bangla language.
• Provide information on Bangladeshi culture, traditions, history, and heritage.
• Create an entertaining and engaging robot that can interact with humans.
• Enable the robot to move motorized, potentially autonomous in the future.
• Add more entertainment and cultural elements in the future.
3. Existing Features
Voice
Currently, the robot has the feature of engaging with a human by asking and answering voice questions. As of now, the robot is able to answer questions in Bangla language, enabling the user to talk with the robot using the local language.
Currently, the response of the robot is functional, although there is a noticeable lag time between the question and the robot’s answer.
Camera & Audio
The robot has a camera sensor. Currently, the camera/audio sensor is used as the robot’s input system for the voice features. At the moment, the voice features do not require a separate microphone.
Display
The front side of the robot features a display screen. Front display is used as part of the robot’s interface for the ShongiBot software while engaging with the user.
Hand Movement
The robot currently has a working hand movement system. The hands can be moved to engage with the user.

5. Current Mobility
ShongiBot is set to become an autonomous robot, and hence potentially mobile. At the moment, the robot does not have wheels or related motorized equipment. Consequently, currently, the robot cannot move itself.
The proposed design is to have 4 wheels and 4 motors with 1 motor per wheel. Wheels and motors will be added in future updates.
Once wheels and motors are added, the robot will be programmed to move forward and backward, and potentially turn and navigate.
6. Autonomous Navigation
The robot will be able to navigate on its own, avoiding obstacles and other objects.
A feature for the robot to avoid obstacles and collisions is planned. An ultrasonic sensor is considered an option for the obstacle detection system.
The idea is to have ShongiBot recognize and avoid obstacles and potential collision objects. Obstacle avoidance will allow the robot to stop, reverse, and change direction when needed.
Navigation autonomy will be enabled once the robot has the required mobility features such as the motors, wheels, motor controller, and sensors.
7. AI and Voice
AI voice is one of the critical features of the robot. The robot already has the ability at present to answer asked questions. The robot is specifically designed to be able to communicate in Bangla to make it more accessible to local Bangladeshis.
The voice recognition system is a work in progress with plans to make the features faster and more efficient. Currently, there is a noticeable lag when interacting with the robot.
8. Cultural Element
The robot is designed to be a cultural companion, not just a standard robot. The robot can be developed to provide information on history, culture, and heritage, including festivals, food, dresses, music, traditional arts, landmarks, notable figures, traditions, and other unique attributes of Bangladesh.
Thus, the robot can provide cultural interaction merged with technology.
9. Songs
The feature to play songs is not available in the current robot prototype. The robot at the moment is not able to play songs.
The robot will be able to play Bangladeshi songs and music in a future update. The robot will be added with a sound or audio playback system and software to enable the feature to play selected cultural and Bangladeshi songs. This feature can be considered an extra entertainment system for the robot.
This feature will be added at a later time when most of the current working features have been perfected.

14. Conclusion
ShongiBot robot is a developing project by Circuit Breakers with the aim of bringing together artificial intelligence, robotics, voice interaction, and Bangladeshi cultural elements into one fascinating product.
As of now, the robot’s prototype has features such as voice interaction, Bangla question-answering, camera/audio input, display, hand movement, and a 3d printed robot enclosure. The robot does not have wheels or motorized drive system, and hence cannot move at the moment. Images in this paper show evidence of the current development progress of the robot hardware and internal electronics.
Songs cannot be played at the moment, although the possibility of adding Bangladeshi music and songs in the future exists. Circuit Breakers team hope to see a future where their robot ShongiBot can become a full autonomous and interactive cultural companion robot.
