1. Project Overview
1.	ShongiBot is a cultural companion robot designed to interact with people, answer questions, communicate through voice, and introduce users to aspects of Bangladeshi culture.
2.	The main goal of ShongiBot is to combine robotics, artificial intelligence, voice interaction, and cultural education into one interactive robot.
3.	The robot is being developed as an autonomous platform, with future plans for motorized movement and obstacle detection.
________________________________________
2. Main Objectives
The main objectives of ShongiBot are:
•	To create an interactive cultural companion robot.
•	To answer users' questions using an AI-powered voice system.
•	To communicate with users in Bangla.
•	To provide information about Bangladeshi culture, traditions, history, and heritage.
•	To create a friendly and engaging robot that can interact with people.
•	To eventually allow the robot to move autonomously.
•	To add additional entertainment and cultural features in future versions.
________________________________________
3. Current Robot Features
The current version of ShongiBot has several working features.
Voice Interaction
The robot currently has a working voice-based question-and-answer system.
Users can speak to the robot, and the system processes the question and generates a response.
The robot is currently able to answer questions in Bangla, allowing users to communicate with it naturally in the local language.
The voice system is functional, although there is currently a small delay between the user asking a question and the robot responding.
Camera and Microphone
The robot has a camera installed.
At the current stage of development, the camera is also being used as the microphone/input device for the voice interaction system.
This allows the robot to receive audio input without requiring a separate dedicated microphone at the current stage.
Hand Movement
The robot currently has working hand movement.
The hands can be controlled to provide physical movement during interaction.
This gives ShongiBot a more interactive and human-like appearance when communicating with users.

4. Current Mobility Status
ShongiBot is designed to eventually become a mobile autonomous robot.
However, the current prototype does not have wheels or motors installed yet.
Therefore, the robot currently cannot drive or move around on its own.
The planned mobility system will use:
•	4 wheels
•	4 motors
•	1 motor for each wheel
The motors and wheels will be added during a future stage of development.
The robot will be able to play bengali folk music or cultural music in future which is currently on the development stage . 
Once programmed, the robot will be able to move forward, backward, turn, and eventually navigate its environment autonomously.
________________________________________
5. Autonomous Navigation
Autonomous navigation is one of the planned future features of ShongiBot.
A sensor system will be added to help the robot detect objects and obstacles in front of it.
An ultrasonic sensor is being considered for obstacle detection.
The purpose of the sensor system will be to allow ShongiBot to:
•	Detect obstacles.
•	Avoid collisions.
•	Decide when to stop.
•	Change direction when an obstacle is detected.
•	Eventually navigate around its environment.
Autonomous movement will be implemented after the motors, wheels, motor controller, and necessary sensors are installed.
________________________________________
6. Artificial Intelligence and Voice System
The AI voice system is one of the main features of ShongiBot.
The current system is already able to receive questions and provide answers.
The robot is particularly designed to communicate in Bangla, making it more suitable for Bangladeshi users.
The system is still being improved, especially the response speed. At the current stage, the robot may take a short amount of time to respond after receiving a question.
Future improvements will focus on making the interaction faster and more natural.

8. Music and Songs
Song and music playback is NOT currently available on ShongiBot.
The current robot cannot play songs.
However, music and Bangladeshi song playback is planned as a future feature.
In a future version, the robot may be upgraded with the necessary audio playback system and software so that it can play selected cultural and Bangladeshi songs.
This feature will be added only after the current core functions of the robot are completed and stable .

12. Conclusion
ShongiBot is a developing robotics project created by Circuit Breakers with the goal of combining artificial intelligence, robotics, voice interaction, and Bangladeshi cultural education.
The current prototype already demonstrates voice-based interaction, Bangla question answering, camera/audio input, and hand movement.
The robot does not currently have wheels or motors, so it cannot move yet. Autonomous movement, obstacle detection, and other advanced features will be implemented in future development stages.
Although ShongiBot cannot currently play songs, Bangladeshi music and song playback is planned as a future feature.
Through continuous development, the Circuit Breakers team aims to turn ShongiBot into an interactive, autonomous, and educational cultural companion robot.

13. Future Vision
The long-term goal is to develop ShongiBot into a fully interactive autonomous cultural companion.
The final version should be able to communicate with people, answer questions in Bangla, move around independently, detect obstacles, interact physically through its hands, and provide cultural information in an engaging way.
Music and Bangladeshi song playback will also be considered as a future entertainment and cultural feature, but it is not part of the robot's current capabilities.
The project will continue to be developed step by step, with the team focusing first on making the existing systems reliable before adding more advanced features.
